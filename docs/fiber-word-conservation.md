# What transport words conserve, and what finite reaction tables add

There is a stronger restriction than the recent
[prime-power reaction bounds](cycle-prime-power-reactions.md): **on any
odd cycle fiber, a coefficient-free word rule that exactly conserves the
lowest-mode spectral sum for every input preserves the entire multiset of
curvature conjugacy classes.** It cannot cause population-changing reactions.

This holds on a single finite fiber. It needs neither a continuum limit,
a word-length bound, reversibility, nor ordered-product preservation.
The distinction is between multiplication-and-inversion words and
state-dependent finite tables, not between fast and slow implementations.

The currently selected triangle reaction tables lie outside this word
class. Each of the twelve has an explicit obstruction to being a word
map. The prime-power spectral reactions have a different obstruction:
they shrink the subgroup generated by the loop tuple, which no bijective
word map on a finite group can do.

These results do not select a physical energy or invalidate the finite
tables as mathematical maps. They identify additional dynamical choices
that should not be attributed to gauge symmetry or transport composition.

## What is a word rule?

A $K$-input, $K$-output word rule has the form

$$T_G(g_1,\ldots,g_K)=(w_1(g),\ldots,w_K(g)),$$

where each $w_j$ is a finite product of input symbols and their inverses.
There are no prescribed nonidentity group constants, conditional branches,
root-selection operations, or dependence on labels in a multiplication
table. Such a rule commutes with every group homomorphism:

$$\phi^{\times K}T_G=T_H\phi^{\times K}.$$

Conversely, if an operation is specified naturally on **all groups** with
this property, evaluate it on the free group's generators. Its outputs
are words, and applying the homomorphism sending those generators to
arbitrary inputs recovers the operation. The all-groups qualification
matters; this is not a classification of every natural operation on
finite groups alone.

The implemented Hurwitz and BoundaryShear candidates are examples.
Conjugation covariance alone is much weaker than homomorphism naturality.
An adjacency-aware graph rewrite or a relation-dependent finite rule may
legitimately lie outside this class. We have not assumed that physical
laws must be word maps.

## Spectral conservation rigidity on one odd cycle

Derive $G=\operatorname{Aut}(C_n)$ from the cycle graph, for odd $n\ge3$.
Its elements can be written

$$g_j:x\longmapsto\sigma_j x+a_j,\qquad
\sigma_j\in\{1,-1\},\quad a_j\in\mathbb Z_n.$$

This is a coordinate description of the derived permutations, not an
inserted continuum gauge group. On the lowest nonconstant Laplacian mode,

$$\varepsilon(g_j)=
\begin{cases}
2-2\cos(2\pi a_j/n),&\sigma_j=1,\\
2,&\sigma_j=-1.
\end{cases}$$

Fix the input sign pattern $\sigma$. Multiplication and inversion show
that each output word has a sign $\sigma'_j$ depending only on $\sigma$
and an angle coordinate $v_j(\sigma)\cdot a$ modulo $n$, where
$v_j(\sigma)$ is an integer vector. Specifically,

$$(\sigma,a)(\tau,b)=(\sigma\tau,a+\sigma b),\qquad
(\sigma,a)^{-1}=(\sigma,-\sigma a).$$

Suppose the spectral sum is conserved for every tuple. After cancelling
the common $2K$ terms, this is

$$\sum_{j:\sigma'_j=1}
\cos\left(\frac{2\pi}{n}v_j(\sigma)\cdot a\right)
=\sum_{i:\sigma_i=1}\cos\left(\frac{2\pi}{n}a_i\right)
\quad\text{for every }a\in\mathbb Z_n^K.$$

At $a=0$, the equation already says that the number of rotation outputs
equals the number of rotation inputs. Thus reflection count is preserved
for each sign pattern.

Now expand the cosines in characters of the finite abelian group
$\mathbb Z_n^K$. Characters at different frequencies modulo $n$ are
linearly independent. The right side has exactly the frequencies
$\pm e_i$ for rotation inputs, each with coefficient $1/2$.
The left side has positive contributions at $\pm v_j(\sigma)$.

No unwanted frequency can cancel against another output term. Hence
each output rotation has

$$v_j(\sigma)\equiv\pm e_i\pmod n$$

for some rotation input $i$, and each such input occurs exactly once.
The identity frequency is absent on the right and cannot occur on the
left. Because $n$ is odd and at least three, $e_i$ and $-e_i$ are distinct.

Consequently the rotation outputs, as a multiset, are the rotation inputs
up to inversion. In odd dihedral groups, inversion stays within each
rotation conjugacy class and all reflections form one class. Together
with reflection-count preservation, this proves

$$\boxed{\{[T_G(g)_j]\}_{j=1}^K=\{[g_j]\}_{j=1}^K.}$$

The proof permits modular frequency aliasing: it identifies frequencies
modulo $n$. Therefore it does not need large $n$ relative to word length.
For even cycles, the argument still preserves rotation classes and total
reflection count, but does not distinguish the two reflection classes.

There is a second consequence. Let $M$ be the exponent-sum matrix of the
words. Output reflection bits equal $M$ times input reflection bits over
$\mathbb F_2$. Their Hamming weight is preserved for every input, so
$M\bmod2$ must be a permutation matrix: apply the property first to
individual basis vectors and then to their pairwise sums.

If one fixed word rule conserves the spectral sum for arbitrarily large
odd cycle sizes, finite Fourier uniqueness also implies that its integer
exponent matrix itself is a signed permutation matrix, not merely one
modulo $n$. Ordered-product preservation then forces all these signs to
be positive. On the commuting rotation sector, such a rule is simply a
permutation of the inputs. This uniform statement is a corollary, not a
hypothesis needed for the finite-fiber theorem.

## Why the triangle reaction tables are not word maps

Use the actual quotient

$$\operatorname{Aut}(C_3)\cong S_3\xrightarrow{\operatorname{sgn}}C_2.$$

Let $r\ne s$ be two reflections. The tuples $(r,r,r)$ and $(r,r,s)$
have identical quotient data $(-1,-1,-1)$. Every one of the twelve selected
reaction tables fixes the first tuple and changes the second to a tuple
containing one reflection, one nonidentity rotation, and one identity.

Their output sign triples differ. Thus none of these maps descends even
to this quotient. Any tuple of words on $S_3$ would descend, since its
image under sign is evaluated from the input signs alone. Therefore
**none of the twelve selected tables can be represented by coefficient-free
group words, even with arbitrarily long words chosen for this fiber.**

The uniform randomized reaction bank has the same obstruction at the
level of transition distributions: the parallel input stays at sign
pattern $(-1,-1,-1)$ with probability one; the mixed input never does.
Consequently it is not a state-independent mixture of word rules either.
State-dependent selection using finer relational data is essential.

This is not an error in the existing charge derivation. The charge
$(q(e),q(R),q(Z))=(0,1,2)$ was derived **after** selecting rules that admit
a strictly positive additive charge. The calculation determines that
charge within the selected bank; it does not show that gauge covariance,
transport composition, or the fiber adjacency alone demands its
conservation. The selection criterion is a physical-model choice that
remains to be justified.

## Why the prime-power spectral exchanges are not bijective word maps

For any finite group $G$, a word map sends $H^K$ into $H^K$ for every
subgroup $H\le G$. If $T_G$ is bijective, its restriction to $H^K$ is
injective and hence surjective. Its inverse therefore also preserves
each $H^K$, even if the inverse has not been expressed as words.

For $y=T_G(x)$, first use $H=\langle x_1,\ldots,x_K\rangle$ and then
$H'=\langle y_1,\ldots,y_K\rangle$. The two inclusions give

$$\boxed{\langle y_1,\ldots,y_K\rangle
=\langle x_1,\ldots,x_K\rangle.}$$

Our prime-power construction violates this equality. Its source rotations
generate a subgroup of order $p^2$, while its target rotations generate
one of order $p$, for every $a\ge2$. The concrete $C_9$ reaction

$$(t_2,t_5,t_8)\longleftrightarrow(t_3,t_3,e)$$

changes the generated rotation subgroup from $C_9$ to $C_3$. Its lookup
involution is reversible and conjugation equivariant, but its inverse
reconstructs rotations outside the subgroup generated by its input. It
cannot be implemented using only multiplication and inversion of those
input transports. Additional fiber structure or relation-dependent
selection is needed.

This does not mean that such structure is illegitimate. The ambient fiber
contains more information than the subgroup generated by a particular
tuple. The sparse reaction construction uses that information; it is not
a universal transport-word law.

## Unfiltered primitives: conserved quantities need not be local energy

Hurwitz $(A,B)\mapsto(ABA^{-1},A)$ preserves every additive class
function because it exchanges classes. BoundaryShear
$(A,B)\mapsto(ABA,A^{-1})$ can change classes, but its additive,
inverse-invariant class charges have a precise restriction.

If $q(g)=q(g^{-1})$, conservation under BoundaryShear gives
$q(B)=q(ABA)=q(BA^2)$. Therefore $q$ factors through the quotient by
the subgroup generated by all squares. That subgroup is characteristic, and
the quotient is elementary abelian of exponent two. Conversely, every
function on this quotient yields a conserved pair sum: the two quotient
labels are swapped. For an odd cycle the quotient is $C_2$, so only
reflection count survives after normalizing $q(e)=0$. All rotation
charges vanish. This statement concerns the pair/shared-edge update, not
the spectator-blind closing-link lift in
[the earlier obstruction](face-energy-obstruction.md).

A useful third comparison is the coefficient-free involution

$$P(A,B,C)=(AB,B^{-1},BC).$$

It is gauge covariant, flat-fixed, product preserving, and commutes with
reversed inversion. Its square is the identity by free-word cancellation.
Yet it has **no nonconstant real-valued additive one-loop charge on any
finite group**, even without assuming the charge is a class function.

Normalize $q(e)=0$. Conservation at $A=C=e$ gives
$q(B^{-1})=-q(B)$. Setting only $C=e$ then gives
$q(AB)=q(A)+q(B)$. A homomorphism from a finite group to
$(\mathbb R,+)$ is zero.

Nevertheless it has exact collective invariants. For a longer ordered
tuple, define partial products $H_i=g_1\cdots g_i$. Acting on
$(g_i,g_{i+1},g_{i+2})$ exchanges $H_i$ and $H_{i+1}$ and leaves every
other partial product unchanged. Adjacent copies of $P$ thus generate
permutations of the internal partial products. At fixed total product,
their orbits are classified exactly by the multiset

$$\{H_1,\ldots,H_{K-1}\}.$$

The positive quantity $\sum_{i=1}^{K-1}\varepsilon(H_i)$ is conserved,
but it depends on nested loops, their ordering, and boundary framing;
it is not an additive elementary-face energy. Absence of a one-face
charge is not absence of all conservation, and finding a positive
invariant does not identify physical energy. This comparison rule is not
installed as new evolution dynamics.

## A constructive decomposition: a relationally gated word rule

One existing triangle table has a short description that does not evaluate
an energy or consult a charge table. Define

$$\mathcal X=\{(A,B,C): A,B,C\ne e,\ A^2=B^2=C^2=e,
\ (A=B)\mathbin{\mathrm{xor}}(B=C)\}.$$

Using the involution $P$ above, define

$$T(w)=\begin{cases}
P(w),&w\in\mathcal X\cup P(\mathcal X),\\
w,&\text{otherwise}.
\end{cases}$$

This is a microscopic matching predicate followed by a transport-word
update. Its implementation only checks nonidentity, involution, and
equality relations; the inverse branch is recognized by checking whether
$P(w)\in\mathcal X$.

The predicate set is closed under common conjugation and reversed
inversion. Since $P^2=1$ and $P$ commutes with these operations, $T$ is a
flat-fixed, gauge- and orientation-covariant involution preserving ordered
product. Each event also preserves the generated subgroup, because it is
either $P$ or the identity on that input. It is not a word map globally:
the matching predicate is an essential part of the operation.

On odd cycles the nonidentity involutions are exactly the reflections.
The active forward moves are

$$(r,r,s)\mapsto(e,r,rs),\qquad
(s,r,r)\mapsto(sr,r,e),\qquad r\ne s,$$

and the corresponding inverse moves. There are $2n(n-1)$ forward
sources and $4n(n-1)$ moved tuples.

Now solve for conserved additive class charges, without using them in
the rule specification. Normalize $q(e)=0$. Every reflection has the
same class charge $c$. The first displayed exchange requires

$$3c=c+q(rs).$$

Every nonidentity rotation of an odd cycle is $rs$ for two distinct
reflections. Hence the full charge space is exactly

$$\boxed{q(e)=0,\qquad q(r)=c,\qquad q(t_k)=2c\quad(k\ne0).}$$

Conversely these charges are conserved by every active exchange, so the
condition is sufficient, not merely necessary. Choosing $c=1$ gives the
existing triangle charge and the same reflection-length charge on every
odd cycle. It still has the discontinuous small-angle behavior identified
in [the charge-compatibility analysis](cycle-charge-compatibility.md);
it has not become the spectral observable or physical energy.

The construction commutes with **injective** group homomorphisms:
injections preserve and reflect the relations in the predicate. In
particular it is compatible with the refinement embeddings

$$\operatorname{Aut}(C_n)\hookrightarrow\operatorname{Aut}(C_{dn}),
\qquad t_k\mapsto t_{dk},\quad r_k\mapsto r_{dk},$$

for odd $n,d$. This refers to embeddings of the fiber automorphism
groups, not autonomous evolution under arbitrary coarse-graining or a
derived spacetime refinement. Quotient maps may erase inequality or
nonidentity and need not commute with the gate.

The complete $C_3$ table is exactly existing selected rule **27**. Exact
conservation calculations give the one-dimensional charge spaces above
on $C_3,C_5,C_9,C_{27}$. All tuples intertwine under the explicit
$C_3\to C_9$, $C_3\to C_{27}$, and $C_9\to C_{27}$ embeddings.
The full twelve-rule bank has not been replaced by this one rule.

**Provenance matters:** this predicate was reconstructed from a bank
previously selected using a positive-charge criterion. Although the new
specification does not calculate or filter on energy, it is not an
independent prediction of the microscopic matching law from bare fiber
adjacency. The progress is an explicit relational decomposition and a
consistent extension across fibers, not removal of every modeling choice.

The [full-bank construction and shared-link experiment](cycle-relational-dynamics.md)
now extend this decomposition to all twelve existing triangle rules.
They distinguish exact trajectory preservation for embedded coarse states
from $1/n$ reaction suppression in uniform full-fiber ensembles.

## Research consequence and exact calculations

For the spectral candidate, replacing finite tables by purely
compositional word primitives cannot produce population-changing reactions
while retaining exact conservation on all inputs. Conversely, generic
reversible word primitives need not conserve any positive face charge.
Symmetry and invertibility do not bridge that gap by themselves.

The relational gate supplies one concrete example of extra primitive
structure and exposes its exact consequences. The physical-model question
remains:
what relational matching, fiber change, or additional dynamical variables
determine rule selection, and what conservation follows from those rules
without filtering for a desired charge? The current finite tables expose
that question; they do not answer it.

The calculation uses free-word cancellation, integer Fourier coefficients
for all sign patterns, exact finite-group charge spaces, all twelve existing
triangle tables, and the saved prime-power exchanges. For $C_3,C_4,C_5,C_9$,
the normalized additive class-charge dimensions are $2,4,3,5$ for Hurwitz;
$1,3,1,1$ for BoundaryShear; and zero throughout for the partial-product
swap. These calculations corroborate the proofs, not replace them or
establish emergent matter.

Word maps and character orthogonality are established mathematical tools;
see [Word Maps in Finite Simple Groups](https://arxiv.org/abs/1901.00836)
and Keith Conrad's [Characters of Finite Abelian Groups](https://kconrad.math.uconn.edu/blurbs/grouptheory/charthyshort.pdf).
No independent novelty review of the present application has been completed.
