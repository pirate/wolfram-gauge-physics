# Released overlap: gauge alignment drives a variance-directed current

We can now release the former external probe into the ordinary dynamics,
without selecting a readout rule. The gauge-alignment preparation produces
different charge fluctuations after one attempted update and different mean
charges after two. There is an exact short-time law explaining this response:

$$
\boxed{\mathbb E[q(2)]-\mathbf1
=\frac{1}{2M}L_v\operatorname{Var}(q(1))
=\frac{4}{M^2}L_v B a.}
$$

Here $M$ is the actual number of operator slots, $L_v$ the vacancy-slot-
weighted face Laplacian, $B$ the face/fan incidence matrix, and $a$ the
initial gauge-dependent reaction-activity mask. The initial face charges
are all one. No potential, continuum force, or diffusion equation was
inserted into the update rules.

This is an exact specialization of the existing microscopic drift and
fluctuation hierarchy, applied to a genuinely coupled region. It is not
a closed long-time hydrodynamic equation or a claim of molecular binding.

## Release the neighbor rather than prescribe its readout

Start with the two preparations from
[the framed overlap calculation](triangle-boundary-framing.md): the
five-reflection word $(1,1,1,2,2)$ and its 20-update twisted word
$(1,1,1,5,5)$, with neighboring reflection $2$ on face 61.

Now include that neighbor in the evolving region. All six faces
$(70,71,10,1,0,61)$ and all six radial edges may change. Only the outer
six-edge ring is held fixed. There are 108 contained operator slots:
36 pair slots and 72 reaction slots. Every attempt chooses uniformly
among them, including no-ops. No special crossing rule is selected.

The center vertex is internal to this enlarged region. Fixing its gauge
frame leaves five independent radial-link variables. Exhausting those
variables at $Q=6$ and full $S_3$ image gives **2,943 boundary-framed
states**. Every transition is calculated through actual shared-edge
surgery, followed only by this interior-vertex gauge fixing. The outer
links and their frames are unchanged.

The initial internal partition and the old probe holonomy are not held
fixed during this evolution. This is not a composition of independent
patch simulations.

## Derive the current law

Write $d_i=q_i-1$, and define

$$m_i=\mathbb E[d_i],\qquad V_i=\mathbb E[d_i^2].$$

Away from zero mean, $V_i$ is a centered second moment, not the variance.
Let $w_{ij}$ count actual vacancy operator slots on the face pair $(i,j)$,
and $(L_v f)_i=\sum_jw_{ij}(f_i-f_j)$.

One vacancy swap contributes

$$q_j\mathbf1_{q_i=0}-q_i\mathbf1_{q_j=0}$$

to the change of $q_i$. Using
$\mathbf1_{q_i=0}=(d_i^2-d_i)/2$, this is exactly

$$\frac12\left[d_i^2-d_j^2-d_i+d_j
+d_jd_i^2-d_id_j^2\right].$$

For a reaction fan, let $\chi_g$ indicate that its charge pattern is a
permutation of $(0,1,2)$. Summed over its twelve rules, the mean charge
increment on a participating face is $-4d_i\chi_g$. An active $(1,1,1)$
fan has zero mean increment because its six output charge permutations
occur equally often. Elastic rules do not change face charges.

Therefore the exact first-moment identity, for arbitrary distributions
of the current full gauge state, is

$$
2M\,[m_i(t+1)-m_i(t)]
=(L_v(V-m))_i
+\sum_j w_{ij}\mathbb E[d_jd_i^2-d_id_j^2]
-8\sum_{g\ni i}\mathbb E[d_i\chi_g].
$$

The calculation verifies this identity on every one of the 2,943 states.
It is not a moment closure: the right-hand side includes higher moments
whose evolution still requires the underlying gauge dynamics.

### Why it simplifies at the first two updates

From any valid all-reflection initial state, every initial charge is one.
After one update, the charge distribution is invariant under simultaneous
$d\mapsto-d$: either no charge changes, or an active fan produces all
six permutations of $(0,1,2)$ with equal weights. Thus $m(1)=0$, and the
cubic and reaction terms in the preceding equation average to zero at
that instant. Hence

$$m(2)=\frac{1}{2M}L_v V(1).$$

Each active fan contributes 8 to the summed one-step squared-charge
increment of each of its faces. If $a_g$ is its initial activity indicator,

$$V(1)=\frac8M B a,$$

giving the boxed identity. This proof uses local rule moments and actual
operator multiplicities; it is not restricted to the six-face example.
The complete two-step identity was additionally checked on all **243**
all-reflection states in this region's fixed-boundary sector.

The mean pair current at this instant flows toward higher variance. That
variance was generated by the gauge-dependent reactions, not supplied
as a force field. The charge-symmetry assumption generally fails after
this response develops, so iterating the boxed formula as a new evolution
rule would change the model.

## Exact response of the released preparations

For the six-face star, $M=108$ and $L_v=2L_{C_6}$, where $L_{C_6}$ is the
ordinary cycle Laplacian. In fan order $(0,1,2,3,4,5)$, the two initial
activity masks are

$$a_{\rm before}=(1,1,0,1,0,1),\qquad
a_{\rm after}=(1,1,0,1,1,0).$$

Both have four active fans. Their difference is spatial, not a change
in total initial reaction activity. With $\Delta=$ after minus before,
the exact one-step variance difference in face order
$(70,71,10,1,0,61)$ is

$$\Delta V(1)=\frac1{108}(-8,0,0,8,0,0),$$

while the one-step mean difference is zero. After two steps,

$$
\Delta\mathbb E[q(2)]
=\frac1{108^2}(-16,8,-8,16,-8,8).
$$

In particular, the former probe face 61 now has mean difference
$8/108^2=1/1458$. This averages **every** two-attempt scheduler word;
it is not conditioned on a successful interaction. The entries sum to
zero, as required by charge conservation.

The charge-square difference after two attempts is

$$\frac1{108^2}(-1376,-104,-168,992,-296,-200).$$

Its sum is $-1152/108^2=-8/81$. Since $Q=6$, the expected rotation-count
difference is half of that, $-4/81$. The two initially identical charge
fields therefore develop different populations as well as different
spatial means under the same unmodified probability law.

## The effect is transient in this finite region

The exact transition graph is connected, symmetric, and has self-loops.
Its stationary distribution is uniform. The rotation-count populations
are 243 states with zero rotations, 1,620 with one, and 1,080 with two,
so the stationary expected rotation count is exactly $140/109$.

Propagating both complete distributions gives:

| Attempts | Total variation between the two full-state distributions |
| ---: | ---: |
| 16 | 0.293319783 |
| 128 | 0.009767839 |
| 256 | 0.000631507 |
| 512 | 0.000003719 |

These are finite-matrix calculations, not trajectory-sampling estimates.
The first twelve response powers are also computed as exact integers with
denominator $108^n$; the longer propagation uses floating-point arithmetic.

The coupled region converts hidden alignment into a real classical
transport response, then loses the distinction in its stationary mixture.
This is not a self-sustaining object. The fixed exterior ring and finite
size also prevent interpreting these numbers as a whole-mesh mixing law,
physical timescale, or continuum force constant.

The next question is whether extended evolving geometry can sustain the
reaction-activity and variance patterns that drive this current, rather
than merely dissipating a prepared pattern. That requires keeping the
higher-moment and gauge-feedback terms, not turning the short-time identity
into an assumed force law.
